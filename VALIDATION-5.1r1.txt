WaffleHouse-Client 5.1r1 — validation
====================================
Date: 2026-08-30

Scope
-----
5.1r1 is a GUI maintenance revision on top of the corrected 5.1 all-platform
baseline that already includes the complete 5.0r20 maintenance merge. It
repairs the desktop Softphone Phone workspace without removing newer 5.1 NINA,
Windows, macOS, Linux/FreeBSD, or Termux work.

Softphone GUI repair
--------------------
- Default window increased to 1260x800 with a 1120x680 minimum.
- Dialer minimum width increased to 405 px.
- READY/live-call status pill is capped at 58 px height.
- Destination moved to its own full-width row.
- Prefix remains compact and no longer competes with Destination.
- Keypad buttons increased to 76x58 with a 12 pt bold digit font.
- CALL/HANG UP controls increased to 132x42.
- Resume/Mute and Blind/Attended Transfer controls receive half-row spans.
- Initial Phone splitter balance changed to 455/585.
