WaffleHouse-Client 3.3r1
Integrated Media Center

The Media Center remains part of the main WaffleHouse-Client application. It supports local media, direct HTTP/HLS streams, SHOUTcast/Icecast radio, playlists, queue transport, volume, shuffle/repeat, and EQ.

YouTube streaming support is intentionally removed. Stop preserves the queue, and Play/Resume after Stop starts the first queued item automatically.
