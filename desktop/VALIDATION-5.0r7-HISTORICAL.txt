      synchronously during application quit.

Sandbox/native-build limitation
-------------------------------
The packaging environment is Linux and does not provide the full Qt6/PJSIP/macOS
Cocoa runtime or Apple SDK. A native linked macOS .app build and visual menu-bar
regression test cannot be claimed here. The Linux dependency audit was exercised
with automatic dependency installation disabled and correctly stopped on missing
sandbox Qt/media development packages rather than modifying the host.
